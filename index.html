Our story

A Fitzroy bakery since 1993

Babka has been baking on Brunswick Street for more than thirty years — cakes, tarts and pastries for the neighbourhood.

Chris has looked after the bakery since August 2023, keeping the same traditional approach and a case that fills every morning.
